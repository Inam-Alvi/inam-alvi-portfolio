Trace how blood is filtered through a realistic nephron. Watch ultrafiltration in the glomerulus, follow the selective reabsorption of water, glucose and salts back into the peritubular capillaries, and observe urine forming in the collecting duct. Colour-coded fate: particles that fade sideways into the capillary network are reabsorbed into the bloodstream; particles that survive the full tubule journey drop into the test tube and are excreted as urine. Red blood cells circulate only inside the glomerulus & peritubular capillaries — too large to cross the filtration barrier.
